# Phase 07.1 Plan 14: Hex balance, half-cell pieces, coverage proof Summary

Production hex balance closure and drawn-piece computation, with an exact atom-based coverage proof and working negative controls. Decision was GO (07.1-07-SUMMARY).

## Commits

- 170af02 test(07.1-14): hex coverage proof, level-jump check and negative controls

## What was built
- `hex/pieces.rs`: `required_splits` (parents plus neighbours of parents, ascending raw), `close` (worklist, inserts only, idempotent), `pieces` (roots whole; unsplit children of split parents whole if centre/single-parent/both-parents-split, otherwise half on the split parent's side; sorted by (raw, variant)), `VARIANT_FULL_HEX = 0`, `VARIANT_FULL_PENTAGON = 1`, `half_variant(diag, side) = 2 + 2*diag + side`, `variant_sectors(v)` (6 / 5 / 3 sectors; halves of one diagonal disjoint, union 6). Sector k is the sector toward `ring(cell)[k]`; the half toward a split parent covers the sector toward that parent's centre child and its two ring neighbours.
- `tests/hex_coverage.rs`: the six planned tests on the production adjacency/mapping/pieces APIs.

## Evidence (cargo test -p planetary-core --test hex_coverage, about 6 s debug)
- Closed+balanced: 40 random sets (30 at maxL 4, 10 at maxL 5), 2,150,400 atoms: uncovered 0, multi-covered 0, max cover 1, max jump 1 (adjacent-piece level diffs: 0: 134,638, 1: 32,786, 2: 0). Pieces 16,017, half pieces 5,897.
- Pentagon distance rule: 12 sets (one per pentagon, maxL 4), 368,640 atoms: 0 violations, max jump 1.
- Total: 52 sets, 2,519,040 atom samples, 0 violations, max jump 1.
- Forced-split ratio of `close`: 3,349 inserts over 750 random picks = 4.47 per pick (random sets); 300 inserts for the 12 pentagon sets.
- Negative controls (all detected): no closure, 40/40 sets with coverage != 1 (max cover 3); dropped half, 40/40 sets leave a hole; duplicated parent, 40/40 sets double-cover; synthetic jump-2 control (whole cell replaced by its L+2 atoms, exact cover) reports max jump 2 with pairs {0: 2644, 1: 608, 2: 18}.
- Finding (as in S3): parents-only closure (no neighbour rule) already gives coverage 1 and jump 1 on these samples (forced ratio 0.93), so the neighbour rule is the conservative balance guarantee, not exercised to failure by random sets. Informational print only; it is not asserted.
- Pentagons: 60 pentagon cells L1..L5 are all centre children; 1,584 pentagon pieces drawn, all variant 1, 0 partial.
- Extra structural check: for levels 0..3, the common neighbours of cell c and ring[k] are exactly ring[k-1] and ring[k+1], which ties the atom sector definition to the CellFrame sector layout.
