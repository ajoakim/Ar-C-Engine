# Phase 07.1 Plan 12: Hex projection and lattice Summary

Production exact-op gnomonic projection and sector sample lattice for the hex topology. The spike S2 mapping is promoted (unrotated canonical icosahedron, skip-zero-weight summation in ascending global vertex order, only `+ - * /`, `sqrt`, `floor`). Decision was GO (07.1-07).

## What was built

- `LatticePoint { face, a, b, c, d }` with `canonical()` (common powers of two removed; edge/vertex points move to the lowest-index face containing them) and `sample_dir`.
- `CellFrame` (`cell_center_point`, `cell_corner_points`, `cell_sample_point` wrappers) on the sample lattice `d = 3*n*2^L`, n even in 2..=64; `ring(c)` gives the CCW neighbour ring (5 for pentagons).
- `footprint(c)` (`lod::Footprint`), `locate(dir, level)` (non-finite or zero dir returns the level's north-pole cell, T-07.1-31), `cell_center_dir`, `vertices()`.
- `adjacency::{Bary, bary_of, owner}` became `pub(super)`; `icosa::at` added (u8 indexing helper). Additive only. `banned_ops` now scans `hex/mapping.rs` whole-file for platform-width integers.

## Test evidence (`tests/hex_mapping.rs`, 9 tests, 8 s debug)

- edges_and_vertices_bit_identical: 183,510 samples, levels 0..=6, 30 edges from both faces, 12 vertices from 5 faces; 0 mismatches.
- shared_cell_borders_bit_identical: 347,820 border samples, every cell and neighbour at L0..=4 (pentagons and diamond wraps included); equal canonical points and dir bits.
- coarse_equals_even_fine: 194,412 samples over 2,000 seeded cells at L0..=10, n=16; coarse sample i equals fine sample 2i exactly.
- locate_round_trip: 23,662 cells (all L<=5, 5,000 each at L16 and L24); NaN/inf/zero handled. Brute force vs face-lattice metric: 0/10,000 mismatches at L5 (118 differ from spherical nearest centre, as expected).
- footprint_contains_samples: 449,820 samples over 512 cells.
- hex_area_ratio: L5 1.8783, L7 1.9638 (bound 2.0).
- golden_hex_dirs: `GOLDEN_HEX_DIRS = 0x6f3bd3fad3344b5f` (53,091 samples). Windows-computed (toolchain 1.99.0); Linux/macOS confirmation pending hosted CI.
- ring_is_a_ccw_cycle (extra), locate brute force (extra, separate test).

## Deviations from Plan

- [Design] `cell_sample_point` is a convenience wrapper over the new `CellFrame` (the frame amortises the ring computation); the plan's `cell_center_point`/`cell_corner_points` signatures are kept.
- [Design] locate does not run the plan's "up to 7 candidates by largest dot" step. Per the S2 findings it uses the gnomonic face-lattice nearest point (validated 0 mismatches); the spherical-nearest variant would disagree with the cell definition used by sampling.
- [Extra] Two additional tests (ring orientation, locate brute force) beyond the 7 named.
