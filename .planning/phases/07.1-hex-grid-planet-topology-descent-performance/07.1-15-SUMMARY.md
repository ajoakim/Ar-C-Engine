## Findings

1. At an L/L+1 border the coarse side is always a hex outer edge and the fine side the diagonal of the edge child (07.1-06 finding 3). "Edges vs diagonals" is therefore reported as coarse side whole cell vs coarse half piece. With the full balance closure (the real `pieces::close`) the half class never occurs (0 of 10,445), as 07.1-06 found; it is exercised by the extra parents-only run (89 junctions).
2. The eps model is loose (gap/skirt about 0.04 at worst), as noted in ADR-0011 caveats; this proof inherits that caveat. Measured deviation at L2 is 0.276 of eps(L), so kappa 0.25 is not wasted at coarse levels, while fine levels are far below it.
3. The ADR-0010 hex amendment (07.1-17) can quote the table above.
