# Phase 07.1 Plan 19: Streaming throughput (DRAFT, Task 1 evidence)

## Harness

- `planetary_app::stream_bench` (`run_stream`, `analyze`, `csv`, `report_text`): real `PlanetaryApp` on the null backend, `settle_each_frame: false`, simulated 1/30 s per frame, after-frame pacing injected by the caller. No clock in the module.
- `DescentScript::fast_free_flight(radius)`: orbit to 10 m above the surface in 8/3 s (about 80 frames at 30 fps), lateral 0.6 rad, pitch 0.35 rad.
- Example: `planetary_flight --bench-stream [--fast] [--settle] [--frame-ms <ms>] [--frames <n>] [--out <csv>]`. Pacing there is `spin_for_ms` (telemetry module, spin + `yield_now`, no sleep) so workers get a real 33 ms slice per simulated frame.
- Tests (`tests/streaming_lag.rs`): pacing is a counted pump (200 `yield_now` calls per frame), no wall clock.
- Metrics are measured at the camera nadir: wanted leaf level vs drawn leaf level (lag), frames from first wanted level L to first drawn level >= L (frames-to-detail), uploads per simulated second.

## Decision gate

Budget stated up front: detail at the camera within 1.5 s = 45 frames, lag <= 1 level once the descent has settled.

HYPOTHESIS CONFIRMED. Frames-to-detail is 198-224 frames (6.6-7.5 s at 30 fps), 4.4-5x over the budget, and the camera is drawn 8-11 levels coarser than wanted during the descent (60-63% of frames have lag > 1). Supply is 70-83 patches/s against caps that allow 120/s at 30 fps (4 uploads/frame): `awaiting_upload` (generated patches waiting for the upload cap) reaches 72-100 and `queued` 20-48, so workers are not the bottleneck (the 33 ms paced and the unpaced control are identical) and the upload and start caps are. Cancel churn and eviction are zero (`cancelled` 0, `evicted` 0), `max_pending` backpressure is not hit (queued max 48 of 256): those candidates are refuted. The raw "throughput ratio" printed by the harness (uploads over distinct leaves ever wanted, 0.04-0.06) overstates the shortfall because transient leaves wanted for a few frames are counted as demand; the settled demand is about 900-1500 held patches, which the caps need 9-17 s to supply.

Request order is (level, raw id), so with a backlog the camera's own chain competes with unrelated coarse patches anywhere on the planet: ordering is also supported by the lag data (the wanted level at the camera advances far ahead of drawn level while patches elsewhere are fetched).

Planned Task 2 changes all stand: adaptive per-frame budget, nearest/error-first ordering, fallback guarantee check. Churn guard is DROPPED (no cancellations observed).
