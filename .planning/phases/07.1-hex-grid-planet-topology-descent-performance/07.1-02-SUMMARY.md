## Deviations from Plan

1. [Rule 1] Noise gradient constant: the plan's G = 1.875*sqrt(3) assumes a quintic fade; the code uses `t*t*(3-2t)` (max slope 1.5), so G = 1.5*sqrt(3) (valid, tighter). The residual formula also includes the factor 2 from `A*(2*fbm-1)` that the plan formula omitted, otherwise the bound would be too small. Validated by `residual_bound_contains_fine_samples`.
2. [Rule 1] Global height range uses `min = sea_level` (heights are floored by compose, tighter than the plan's min with -(A_b+A_d)) and `max = max(A_b+A_d, sea_level)`. The horizon occluder is `radius + sea_level`.
3. [Rule 1] Trim: the plan's "remove deepest-level splits first" removed the tip of the chain under the camera (it is the only deepest-level split), so level 16 was never reached once the budget was hit. Replaced by removing the lowest-rho split that is `removable` (no split child, and no split level+1 cell touching a child from outside); deepest-level splits are always removable so it still terminates and keeps balance/partition.
4. [Rule 2] The cell under the camera is exempt from frustum culling (`eye_in_cone`), per the must-have.
5. Node budget accounting: cells are evaluated when pushed (`nodes_visited` counts evaluations); a split whose 4 children would exceed the budget stops traversal (`node_budget_hit`).
6. Residual and derived range are always widened (also for the cell's own measured range) since sample min/max do not bound interior points.
7. Golden payload hash in tests/mesh.rs updated (format change); `check-canary`, `check-deps` unaffected. `View`/`LodModel`/`PatchMesh` literals in tests gained the new fields.
8. Added test `reference_stats_report` (prints numbers; not in plan).
