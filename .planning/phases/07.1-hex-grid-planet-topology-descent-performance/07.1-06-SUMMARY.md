## Findings that deserve attention

1. **The neighbour rule of the balance closure is not needed for S3 as measured.** Closing only under "all parents of a split cell are split" (no neighbours of the parents) also gives exact coverage and max jump 1: 0 violations in 40 sets, and in 300 extra stress sets (maxL 4). The research expectation "jump 2 without the rule" is not reproduced, with the jump detector itself shown to work (synthetic control reaches 2). A structural argument agrees: every neighbour of a split cell is a child of one of its (split) parents, hence drawn at the same level or split. The cost difference is large: 0.97 forced splits per random pick (parents only) vs 4.58 (full rule). The full rule has one effect found: it makes a half piece never face finer cells (0 coarse half-piece borders with full closure vs 147 junctions with parents-only). This is an empirical result on random sets, not a proof; 07.1-07 should decide whether to keep the stricter rule only for the simplicity of S4's single junction class.
2. **S4 passes with a ~27x to 1000x margin, which says the eps model is very loose, not that the geometry is tight.** `eps(L) = 0.25 * R * a_L` is far above the real deviation of this terrain (level 0 gap is dominated by chord sagitta s^2/8R, deeper levels by the roughness of the 14-octave detail layer, which is far below kappa*spacing even at 1 m). The plan's bound is therefore satisfied trivially; it does not validate the 1.5 margin against the engine's real `LodModel` error measure.
3. **Junction geometry is a single object.** A coarse hex outer edge (N+1 samples, step 2 fine units) is exactly the diagonal of the fine edge child (2N+1 samples). A fine hex outer edge never faces a coarse piece, and a half piece's diagonal only ever faces a coarser piece (so it appears as the fine side of that junction). "Edges vs diagonals" are therefore reported as the coarse side being a whole cell vs a half piece.
4. **Test power.** S3 sets that reach their maximum level: 40/40; half pieces exercised; pentagons and icosahedron-edge junctions exercised in S4 (asserted > 0).

## Deviations from Plan

- [Method] S3 coverage uses exact atoms (6 per lattice triangle, nested 4:1 dyadically; verified both as a partition and geometrically) instead of the plan's centroid sample points at level maxL+2 with integer orientation tests. Equivalent in strength (an exact partition, 0 ties by construction) and avoids unfolding across faces. Closure and piece rules are as in the plan; roots are all split for even seeds, one random root for odd seeds.
- [Additive] Extra negative controls beyond the plan: parents-only closure variant and a synthetic jump-2 exact cover (the plan's no-closure control alone cannot show the jump detector works, since it breaks coverage rather than jump). Extra test `s4_deep_levels_gap_report` (L3-19 via integer-only construction) because the table-based hierarchy is limited to level 5.
- [Scope] Real-hierarchy S4 covers coarse levels 0-4 (topology tables built to level 5); deeper levels use the table-free interior construction (no pentagons or face seams there).
