# Phase 07.1 Plan 03: Streaming wiring and crash-path fixes Summary

Resident height ranges and frustum planes now drive selection, draws are culled and can no longer be dropped silently, and wgpu asynchronous errors surface as `RenderError` instead of a panic.

## Commits

- 7b44ce3 Task 1: range-aware select, draw budget, draw culling, frustum planes, telemetry
- b228f8a Task 2: wgpu error capture, `RenderError::DeviceLost`, hover bound test

## What changed

- `HeldPatch.range` from `mesh.height_min/max`; `prepare` passes `held.get(&id).map(|h| h.range)` to `select`.
- `TerrainSystem::new` rejects `max_leaves < 6` or `> MAX_DRAWS_PER_FRAME - DRAW_MARGIN` (DRAW_MARGIN = 24).
- `gpu_frame` culls effective-cut leaves (horizon, frustum, eye cell exempt) using the last view. Culled leaves stay in `drawn()` (partition semantic unchanged); `draw_items + culled_draws == drawn_leaves`. Overflow returns `SdkError::App("draw budget exceeded: ..")`. `draw_overflow` removed everywhere.
- `TerrainReport` gained `pending_requests`, `draw_items`, `culled_draws`; telemetry columns replaced `draw_overflow` with these three (34 columns).
- `PlanetaryCamera::frustum_planes(aspect, near)`: near, left, right, bottom, top, disabled far; unit normals, sqrt only. The app attaches it to the view with a conservative aspect (never narrower than 16:9; real width tracked from `Resized`).
- wgpu backend: `device.on_uncaptured_error` and `set_device_lost_callback` store the first error in an `Arc<Mutex<Option<RenderError>>>`, logged with `tracing::error!`; `submit` returns it before and after rendering. Generic engine code, no planetary wording under crates/.

## Held-meshes growth while hovering

Baseline: 1186 -> 1534 over 1140 frames (max 1744), growing. Now (3000-frame release bench, camera hovering from frame ~480): held 354 (f600), 535 (f800), 765 (f1000), 850 (f1500) and stays at 850 through f3000, with wanted == drawn == 573 and pending 0. So growth is the rate-limited convergence (4 uploads, 8 starts per frame) and it plateaus; it is bounded by the residency class budgets (base patches + `detail_budget_patches`). Test `held_meshes_bounded_while_hovering` asserts held, pending and pending_requests stay within that bound over 600 frames of a drifting eye. No evictions were needed. The 8-start/4-upload caps were left at their defaults (plan scope); at hover the 850 meshes take ~1000 frames to arrive.
