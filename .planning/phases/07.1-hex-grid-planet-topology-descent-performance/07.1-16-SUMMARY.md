# Phase 07.1 Plan 16: HexSphere implements Topology Summary

`HexSphere` is the second `Topology`, proven by one generic contract suite run for both implementations, generic selection at the real defaults, and an exact coverage check on 30 real cuts. The cube-only `lod::select` wrapper and the v2 payload are removed. One must-have is not met: see BLOCKER.

## BLOCKER (RESOLVED in 07.1-16b: parents-only closure chosen by the user; level 16 now reached, see 07.1-16b-SUMMARY.md; the numbers below are the superseded full-closure measurements)

Must-have "generic select_cells over HexSphere reaches level 16 at the look-at point from 3 m within the 900-piece budget" is not met at a generic point. Under the ADR-0011 full balance closure (parents plus all neighbours of the parents) the selection is budget-bound, not search-bound.

| Evidence | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Pure chain of splits at one point (closed), pieces to reach level L | L5 319, L8 558, L10 813, L12 981, L13 1110, L15 1260 (about 80 pieces per level) |
| Same chain with a parents-only closure plus a neighbour ring per level (measured, not implemented) | L5 179, L10 329, L15 479 (about 30 per level) |
| Uncapped selection at 3 m (generic point) | 2024 pieces, level 16 |
| Capped at 900 | deepest 12 at the generic point, 14 at an icosahedron edge, 16 at a pentagon |

The test `hex_selection_reaches_max_level` holds the original assertion and is `#[ignore]`d with the reason (not weakened). Options: (a) relax the closure to parents-only (07.1-06 measured max jump 1 and exact coverage without the neighbour half; 07.1-15 proved seams for the half-facing-finer case, 89 junctions, 0 violations), which changes ADR-0011's balance rule and `pieces::close`; (b) raise `max_leaves` for hex; (c) accept level 12 to 14 at 3 m. This is an architectural change (Rule 4), so it was not made.

## What was built

- `hex/topology.rs`: `HexSphere::new(n) -> Option<HexSphere>` (n even, 2..=64, no panics, T-07.1-40), all trait methods delegate to the hex modules, variant index = position in `hex_index_variants`, `sample_angle0 = sqrt(8*PI/(10*sqrt(3))) / (sqrt(3)*n)`, name `"hex"`.
- `Topology::eager_closure()` (default false, hex true) and `select_cells` changes (Rule 1 and 3 fixes found by the real-parameter run):
  - `removable()` (trim) was cube-shaped; for hex it removed splits that the closure requires again (debug assertion "split not closed", 121 cells). It is now generic: no split child, and no split child of a neighbour whose `required_splits` contains the cell.
  - A hex cell queued through two parents was split and re-evaluated repeatedly (820k node visits). Cells are now refined once (`expanded`); a cell forced earlier only has its children evaluated.
  - Eager mode charges the forced group and an exact piece delta (unsplit roots plus unsplit children of split cells) at split time, so the 900 budget is exact (no trimming; before: 108 trimmed splits).
  - Cube goldens (`cube_select_matches_pre_refactor_goldens`, hash 0xee2da36546c6e081, 131 digests) unchanged.

## Evidence: contract and coverage

- `trait_contract_holds_for_both` (cube and hex; all cells to level 5 plus 40 random descents to level 12): from_raw round trip, children one level deeper, c in parents(child), primary parent = min parent, parents ascending, neighbours reciprocal and same level, required_splits strictly shallower and containing the parents, locate(footprint centre) = cell, closure fixpoint.
- `meshes_and_payload_v3_hold_for_both`: mesh vertices inside the footprint cone, payload v3 round trip, wrong id and truncation rejected, for both.
- `hex_variants_match_pieces`: 8 variants; the variants of class 0 together use exactly `vertex_count_hex(16)` vertices and class 1 `vertex_count_pentagon(16)`; pieces of real selections use existing variants of the right class (pentagon pieces only class 1).
- `coverage_of_selected_cuts`: 30 real cuts at 1 km (12 pentagon centres, 18 icosahedron-edge midpoints), lazily generated atoms of the cut's deepest level + 2 inside a disc of 3 cell spacings: 236,064 atoms, 0 uncovered, 0 multi-covered, max level jump 1, 4,612 level-diff-1 pairs. Negative control (drop the half piece nearest the eye, 12 views): 1,152 violations detected.
