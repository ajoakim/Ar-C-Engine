- ADR-0008: exact-op arithmetic in selection and slack; BTreeMap/BTreeSet or sorted Vec only in deterministic paths (no HashMap); `usize` stays out of BORDER-CRITICAL regions; planetary-core banned_ops stays green.
- Results must stay bit-identical: skipping and caching never change the selection, only whether it is recomputed (equality test, not a tolerance).
- Skip validity (decided, conservative): a skip is allowed only if ALL hold: the last selection hit no node budget, leaf budget or trim (`!node_budget_hit && !leaf_budget_hit && trimmed_splits == 0`); `proj_scale`, `tau`, `hysteresis`, `max_level` and the presence of frustum planes are unchanged; no upload or eviction changed `held` since the last selection (a `held_version` counter, because resident ranges feed `range_for`); the eye moved by `dE` and the frustum plane normals rotated by at most `alpha` radians (alpha = 1.5708 * max over the 6 planes of |n_new - n_old|, conservative for chord vs arc) with `dE + alpha * D_max < margin_min`, where `margin_min` is the minimum over ALL evaluated cells of the distance margin to any decision boundary (SSE threshold crossing in distance, horizon angle margin converted to metres at the eye's distance to the occluder sphere, signed plane margin of the cell bound vs each frustum plane, in-cone test margin) and `D_max` is the maximum over evaluated cells of `|centre - eye| + bound radius`; every margin is computed so that a move of dE in the eye changes the corresponding test value by at most dE (1-Lipschitz), and a rotation by alpha changes plane values by at most alpha * D. When any margin cannot be bounded, it is 0 (never skip). A teleport (dE larger than the previous cell radius scale or any non-finite) re-selects.
- Residency-driven state stays correct: the effective cut and adaptive caps run every frame in `gpu_frame`/`StreamBudget::next` regardless of skipping; only `select_cells` (and the scored/closure work that depends only on its result and `held`) may be skipped, and `plan_requests_scored` still runs every frame because residency changes between frames.
