# Phase 8 Plan 05: ECS spike, ADR-0017 and ADR amendments Summary

A standalone `spikes/ecs-bench` tooling crate measures `engine_world::World` against hecs 0.11.2 at N = 1e4 and 1e5, and ADR-0017 records "no ECS in P0" with those release numbers; ADRs 0005, 0006 and 0010 gain append-only Phase 8 amendments.

## ECS headline numbers (median of 3 release runs, `--quick`)
| case | N | engine World | hecs |
|------|---|--------------|------|
| update_all_locals | 1e5 | 3.96 ms | 92.10 us (43x) |
| hierarchy_update | 1e5 | 5.31 ms | 2.19 ms (2.4x) |
| spawn | 1e5 | 10.57 ms | 6.01 ms (1.8x) |
| random_1pct | 1e5 | 52.14 us | 7.92 us (6.6x) |
| despawn10_respawn | 1e5 | 1.01 ms | 533.82 us (1.9x) |

hecs is faster everywhere; the engine costs about 40 ns per entity (sweep) and 53 ns (hierarchy), below 0.5 ms per case at 1e4. Decision: keep `World`; revisit at roughly 4e4 to 5e4 dynamic entities per frame, multi-component queries or system scheduling.

## Decisions Made
- No ECS in P0; `engine_world::World` stays (ADR-0017). hecs confined to `spikes/ecs-bench`.
- ADR-0006 hash grid kept; crossover near N = 100-300 (region/radius), 300-1000 (frustum); updates 8x-24x slower than Linear.
- ADR-0005: software-adapter evidence recorded; hosted Linux/macOS goldens explicitly not yet present (plan 11).
- ADR-0010+0014 together are the LOD transition decision; hosted run 45947523739 closes the pending cross-OS golden notes, with no hashed morph golden (invariant tests only).

## Deviations from Plan
- The plan's `cargo bench --benches -- --noplot --quick` form was replaced by `--bench ecs` (flag fails on lib test targets, per plan 04 note).
- The bench uses three runs in separate processes for medians (plan 04 method) rather than one.
- ADR-0014 text itself was not edited; its pending-CI note is closed by a statement in the ADR-0010 amendment (scope listed only 0005/0006/0010).
- The hecs hierarchy case does less work than the engine's (children's world transforms are computed but not stored); stated in the ADR and results.
